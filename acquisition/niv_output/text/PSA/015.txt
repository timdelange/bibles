Psalm 15

1 A psalm of David.Lord, who may dwell in your sanctuary?Who may live on your holy hill?
2 He whose walk is blameless and who does what is righteous,who speaks the truth from his heart
3 and has no slander on his tongue, who does his neighbor no wrongand casts no slur on his fellowman,
4 who despises a vile man but honors those who fear the Lord,who keeps his oatheven when it hurts,
5 who lends his money without usury and does not accept a bribe against the innocent.He who does these thingswill never be shaken.
