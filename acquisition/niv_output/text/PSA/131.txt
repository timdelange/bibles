Psalm 131

1 A song of ascents. Of David.My heart is not proud, O Lord,my eyes are not haughty;I do not concern myself with great mattersor things too wonderful for me.
2 But I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with its mother,like a weaned child is my soul within me.
3 O Israel, put your hope in the Lord both now and forevermore.
